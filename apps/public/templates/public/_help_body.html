Help

Signing in

There are no passwords. To join, enter your email address on the create an account page and we'll send you a link and a six-digit code. Use either one within 15 minutes and your account is made, with a starter library of exercises and a sample workout. You'll then be offered a passkey, so next time you sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN.

After that, sign in on the sign-in page with your passkey, or with a new link and code.

If you've installed the app on an iPhone or iPad, type the code into the app rather than opening the link: the link signs in Safari, not the app.

Passkeys

Once you're signed in, open Account and choose Add a passkey. From then on you can sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN, using Sign in with a passkey.

Building a workout

Open Manage, then New workout. Add exercises from the library on the left; they play in that order. Set the rest between exercises, the number of rounds and the break between rounds. The panel on the right shows the whole sequence, its length, and the mix of types and muscles. One-sided exercises run twice, once per side, with a short switch between.

Pinning and archiving workouts

Pin the workouts you do most and they're listed first on your phone, by name. The rest wait behind Show all workouts, newest first. Archive a workout to take it off the phone without deleting it; Restore brings it back. Pin, Archive and Restore are on each workout in Manage. Workouts Claude made for you that are never done or changed for seven days are archived; pin one to keep it.

Running a workout

Open Workout on your phone and pick a workout. After a five-second get-ready, each exercise and rest fills the screen with its countdown, with beeps for the last three seconds. Tap to pause; from the pause screen you can go back, skip or end. At the end, rate how hard it was if you like.

What is logged

Only time actually worked is logged, including when you end a workout early. Time on an exercise with several types is split evenly between them; it counts in full for every muscle the exercise works. A workout you discard from the finish screen isn't logged.

Seeing what you've trained

Open Report for this week or this month: time worked, sessions, active days and effort against the period before, then the time split by type, by muscle group and by movement, your top exercises, and the types and muscles you haven't trained. Step back with Previous, or pick a bar in the trend. Report needs a connection.

Installing the app

On iPhone or iPad, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Install in the browser's menu.

Using it offline

Once you've opened Workout while signed in, your workouts are saved on the phone and it opens without a connection. Finished workouts are kept on the phone and sent when you're back online.

Claude

  1. On claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app, open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Choose +, then Add custom connector.
  3. Give it the name {{ pwa.name }}, paste the address into the URL field, leave Advanced settings empty and choose Add.
  4. Choose Connect. Claude opens the {{ pwa.name }} sign-in page: enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it.
  5. Start a new chat and ask. If Claude doesn't use {{ pwa.name }}, open the + menu under the message box, choose Connectors and turn {{ pwa.name }} on.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT adds connectors through developer mode, on the web only. Business, Enterprise and Edu plans can use every tool; Pro can use only the ones that read. In a workspace, an admin turns developer mode on, or allows you to.

  1. In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Apps, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps, choose Create. Give it the name {{ pwa.name }}, paste the address and choose OAuth.
  3. Choose Scan Tools. ChatGPT opens the {{ pwa.name }} sign-in page: enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it. Then choose Create.
  4. {{ pwa.name }} is now under Settings, Apps, marked Dev. Start a new chat and ask.

It can read your library and log, and add or change exercises and workouts, but never delete anything. Your AI chat sees what your account holds, and nothing of anyone else's. To disconnect it, remove the connector in your AI chat's settings, or open Account, then Connected apps.
