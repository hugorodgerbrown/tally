Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

What we keep

  • Your email address, to identify your account and send you sign-in links.
  • Sign-in requests: when you asked to sign in, and whether you did. Deleted once they expire.
  • Passkeys: a public key for each one you add, its name, and when it was last used. Your device keeps the private key; we never see it, or your face or fingerprint.
  • Your library: the exercises and workouts you make or change.
  • Your training log: each workout you do, when you started and finished it, the time worked on each exercise, and the effort score if you give one.

On your device

The app stores your workouts, pages you've opened and any finished workouts not yet sent, so it works without a connection. Signing out removes the saved pages.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, and one protects forms from forgery. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Claude and other apps

If you connect Claude, it reads and changes your library and log on your behalf, under Anthropic's terms. Nothing is shared with it until you approve the connection, and you can disconnect it at any time.

Who else sees it

Our hosting and email providers process data on our behalf. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else. Other accounts never see your library or log.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and everything in it to be deleted.
